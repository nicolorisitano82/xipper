# 8. Finder, Quick Look, Shortcuts and the menu bar

## The Xipper menu in the Finder

Enable **Xipper in the Finder** in **System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Finder Extensions**. Xipper offers to open that page on first launch, and from **Settings › Integrations**.

Right-click files or folders in the Finder, or use the Xipper button in the toolbar. The fitting action comes first:
- **Decompress “name”** on archives;
- **Open in Xipper** on BAM and BCF files, which are browsed rather than extracted;
- **Compress “name”** on anything else.

The other actions are under **More with Xipper**:
- **Compress**, also for archives;
- **Decompress the archives**;
- **Verify**;
- **Open in Xipper**;
- **Mount as a Volume**.

Finder actions always create new files next to the originals and never overwrite anything. Progress shows in Xipper's window, or in the menu bar if the icon is on.

## Quick Actions and Services

Even without the extension, the Finder's **Quick Actions** and the **Services** menu offer:

- **Compress with Xipper**
- **Extract with Xipper**
- **Verify with Xipper**

They use the format and settings of **Settings › General**.

## Quick Look

Select an archive in the Finder and press the space bar to see its contents without opening Xipper. Archives also get thumbnails in the Finder.

## Shortcuts

The Shortcuts app gets four Xipper actions:

| Action | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Compress with Xipper** | Files and folders, format, name | The archive |
| **Extract with Xipper** | An archive, destination, password | The extracted folder |
| **Verify with Xipper** | An archive, password | Whether it is intact, and a report |
| **List Contents with Xipper** | An archive | The list of entries |

Some ideas:
- compress a folder every evening;
- verify every archive that lands in Downloads;
- extract attachments saved by Mail.

## The menu bar icon

**Settings › Integrations › Menu bar icon** shows running jobs in the menu bar, with their progress, and opens Xipper.

## Default app

On first launch, Xipper offers to become the default app for archives. You can change this later in the Finder's **Get Info › Open with**, or from the Welcome steps (**Help › Welcome to Xipper**).
